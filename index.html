About Us & Institutional Heritage

Who We Are: Specialized Cost Intelligence & Commercial Advisory

iCMA Consulting is a specialized management accounting, cost intelligence, and commercial advisory firm. We partner with manufacturing enterprises, infrastructure and EPC developers, public-sector undertakings, and autonomous institutions to solve complex challenges in cost estimation, commercial pricing, capital project appraisal, and operational cost optimization.

Traditional accounting looks backward at money already spent. At iCMA Consulting, we focus on forward-looking cost analysis and strategic decision support—enabling leadership teams to forecast pre-production and project costs accurately, evaluate Make-or-Buy trade-offs, structure resilient pricing, and identify cost and schedule risks early.
